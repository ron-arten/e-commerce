Menus

A focused demo of a collapsible left-side navigation. The sidebar starts collapsed — hover any icon to preview its submenu in a flyout panel. Use the toggle at the top to expand the sidebar; in expanded mode, click any parent item to open an inline dropdown.

Tab key navigates the items, Escape collapses the sidebar (or closes any open flyout).

This demo is designed for desktop interactions (hover and click). Try it on a larger viewport.
